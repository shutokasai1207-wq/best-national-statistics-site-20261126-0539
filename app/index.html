Nippon in Numbers — an independent presentation of Japan's official statistics. Figures come from the Statistics Bureau of Japan, the Cabinet Office, the Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare, the Ministry of Finance and the National Tax Agency. This site is not affiliated with any government body. All figures are labelled with their source and year on the page. Figures last checked November 2026.

Built as a static site: no tracking, no network requests, no external fonts or images.
